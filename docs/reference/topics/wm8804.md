# WM8804 S/PDIF transceiver

## Summary

The WM8804 is the selected digital-audio interface component for an S/PDIF input
path. It can recover clock and audio from S/PDIF and expose the audio through a
serial digital audio interface such as I2S.

## Confirmed facts

- It provides one S/PDIF receive channel and one transmit channel with an internal
  jitter-attenuating PLL.
- The audio interface supports I2S, left/right-justified, and DSP formats at
  16/20/24-bit word lengths and 32-192 ksample/s.
- Digital and PLL supplies are 2.7-3.6 V.
- The package is a 20-lead SSOP; the datasheet shows a nominal body of approximately
  7.2 mm x 5.3 mm x 1.75 mm.
- Software control is available over two- or three-wire interfaces; hardware mode
  is also supported.
- Cirrus Logic lists the WM8804 as discontinued.

## Constraints

- Treat lifecycle status as a sourcing risk and validate available stock,
  authenticity, and a replacement strategy before freezing a production design.
- The chip requires a complete receiver/carrier design: S/PDIF input conditioning,
  clocks, power/decoupling, control, logic compatibility, PCB layout, and the I2S
  connection are not defined by component selection alone.
- The new [eARC/ARC working architecture](digital-audio-input.md) must route
  uncompressed HDMI multichannel audio around the S/PDIF-only path. WM8804 may
  remain useful for optical/coaxial inputs; a selected HDMI receiver might also
  provide this reception function. No decision to replace the WM8804 has been
  made. Its non-audio detection does not decode Dolby/DTS audio.
