# WM8804 S/PDIF transceiver

## Summary

The WM8804 was the original digital-audio interface candidate for an S/PDIF input
path. A10/A11 use CS8416K as a draft alternative; this does not erase the earlier choice or qualify the replacement. It can recover clock and audio from S/PDIF and expose the audio through a
serial digital audio interface such as I2S.

## Confirmed facts

- It provides one S/PDIF receive channel and one transmit channel with an internal
  jitter-attenuating PLL.
- The audio interface supports I2S, left/right-justified, and DSP formats at
  16/20/24-bit word lengths and 32-192 ksample/s.
- Digital and PLL supplies are 2.7-3.6 V.
- The package is a 20-lead SSOP; the datasheet shows a nominal body of approximately
  7.2 mm x 5.3 mm x 1.75 mm.
- Software control is available over two- or three-wire interfaces; hardware mode
  is also supported.
- Cirrus Logic lists the WM8804 as discontinued.

## Constraints

- Treat lifecycle status as a sourcing risk and validate available stock,
  authenticity, and a replacement strategy before freezing a production design.
- The chip requires a complete receiver/carrier design: S/PDIF input conditioning,
  clocks, power/decoupling, control, logic compatibility, PCB layout, and the I2S
  connection are not defined by component selection alone.
- The [ARC-first architecture](digital-audio-input.md) uses stereo/encoded-surround carriers. Future eARC multichannel PCM would need to bypass any S/PDIF-only path. A10/A11 propose CS8416K instead of WM8804, but no production replacement is qualified. Non-audio detection does not decode Dolby/DTS audio.

## A10 electrical draft update

See the [A10 work log](../../worklog/2026-11-03-a10-electrical-draft.md). A separate KiCad schematic and rigid integration model now exist. CS8416K is the draft optical/coax choice, with documented source selection and LVDS circuitry. HDMI remains unwired in A10. [Decision 0014](../../decisions/0014-use-arc-for-first-version.md) now targets ARC first and defers eARC; at that scope-record stage no ARC implementation existed. The subsequent [A11 candidate](../../worklog/2026-11-04-a11-arc-electrical-study.md) adds ARC into CS8416 selection; no order-ready BOM or manufacturing release exists. Prior WM8804 and connector records remain historical evidence.
